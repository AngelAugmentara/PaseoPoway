The architecture

Built around the paseo.

Three buildings along Poway Road are connected by the paseo that gives the community its name. With parking tucked underground, the ground level is dedicated to people, creating a walkable environment where restaurants and shops line both Poway Road and the paseo. Live-work homes feature their own storefronts, creating a natural connection between home and community.

Above the shops, the buildings open into landscaped courtyards, while a second-floor terrace sits alongside the residents’ Commons Room. Select townhomes are complemented by private rooftop terraces, offering an elevated space to relax and take in the surroundings.
